📱 How to program NFC tags for Café Coffea

  1. Download the free NFC Tools app (iOS App Store or Google Play Store).
  2. In the app, choose Write → Add a record → URL / URI.
  3. Copy the Table URL from any card below (click "Copy NFC Payload") and paste it into the URL field.
  4. Tap Write and hold your smartphone against the table's NFC sticker or puck (e.g. NTAG213 / NTAG215).
  5. Test the tag: tapping with any modern iPhone or Android immediately opens the menu at that exact table!
